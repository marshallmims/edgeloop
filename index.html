EdgeLoop reads any sensor that broadcasts the standard Bluetooth Heart Rate service. Then click HR Monitor → Scan & Pair.

Watches
  • Pixel Watch / Wear OS: native broadcast. Quick Settings → Heart Rate Sharing, then Scan & Pair.
  • Galaxy Watch: install the "Heart for Bluetooth" app on the watch and start its broadcast.
  • Apple Watch: broadcast with HeartCast or Echo to EdgeLoop on a second device (PC, Mac, tablet). iOS cannot loop back on the same iPhone; in Bluefy on the iPhone, pair a chest strap instead.
  • Budget GloryFit / Da Fit watches do not broadcast the Heart Rate service and cannot work.
Chest straps
  • Polar H10, Coospo H6M, Cycplus H2 Pro, Garmin, Wahoo: pair directly; the most stable option.
  • Wet the electrodes before wearing, or the strap reports nothing or poor contact.
Browsers
  • Chrome / Edge on Windows, macOS, Android and ChromeOS: works out of the box. iPhone / iPad: use Bluefy.
  • Linux Chrome: enable chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features and use a native (non-Flatpak / non-Snap) browser, or Bluetooth stays hidden.
  • Firefox and Safari do not support Web Bluetooth.

Step 1 of 3

Connect a heart rate monitor

Tap HR Monitor and Scan & Pair a BLE strap or watch. Chest straps (Polar H10, etc.) stay more stable than optical sensors. You can also use the built-in HR simulator while testing.

Apple Watch cannot loop back on the same iPhone. Broadcast HeartCast/Echo to EdgeLoop on a PC, Mac, or tablet, or use a chest strap.

Step 2 of 3

Connect your toys

Three ways in: The Handy with its Wi-Fi connection key; Intiface Central at ws://localhost:12345 for vibrators and Bluetooth toys; or TCode Serial for OSR2, SR6 and OSSM strokers straight over USB (Chrome or Edge on a desktop, no Intiface needed). The heart-rate simulator in the HR Monitor modal stands in for a sensor, not for a toy.

In the TCode or Intiface tray, assign each axis Primary, Secondary, or OFF, then tap Test so you know Linear (stroke) and Rotate (twist/roll) are mapped. The Hardware Travel Envelope (Handy or TCode modal) bounds every stroke.

Step 3 of 3

Set your ceiling and start

Enter Resting HR and Climax HR, then open Session Setup for duration, warm-up, and guards. Pick a mode (or a game) and press Start when both HR and a toy are ready.

The engine throttles automatically. If you go over, tap Came Early so local bio-learning lowers the working ceiling next time. Two guards watch the sensor: the signal-loss timeout (3-20 s) pauses the motors when readings stop, and auto-resume picks the session back up when they return.
