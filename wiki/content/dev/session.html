This page is 1.1.4.31 on dev.edgeloop.app. edgeloop.app is still 1.1.3. The two sites do not share a pairing. What follows is how a session is set up on the dev player, and why the controls sit where they do.

How the page is arranged

The heart-rate card is the session you are about to run. Warm-up, Resting, Single max, Dual max, Calibrate, Length, and the minute window are matching tiles on that card. They stay there with the Player open or closed. The big number under the tiles is the live pulse. With nothing linked it shows --. 70 is the resting default in its own tile. It is not a pulse. Disconnecting the strap blanks the readout again.

Edges and pauses sit on that same card, next to the pulse. Heart rate, The Handy, Intiface, VacuGlide, and T-Code stay in one row across the top, on the main page and inside the Player. On a narrow window the row scrolls. It keeps every device showing. The line above the row asks for a heart-rate monitor and one toy, and that line goes away once both are connected. Open a device to connect it. Connect a toy is that step.

Play Style and Session Endgame share one row. The two headings sit on the same line, and the two cards start and end together. One click opens both lists, so a short card does not sit beside a tall one. Picking a play style or an ending rolls both lists up. The card keeps that choice and its description. Edge Training and No Nut November keep their settings on the endgame card.

With no game selected, the endgame is Climax, Soft Landing, or Strict Denial. That is what happens when the session timer runs out. Survival keeps going until you press Stop. A session length does not end it. The Oracle decides its own finish, and it keeps whichever of those three you had selected. No Nut November ends at the day's edge quota. The toys ease down and the session ends. There is no orgasm, and there is no denial percent. The row that says "When the timer runs out" shows on Edge Training, which still uses those three.

Length is Fixed, Mystery, or Endless, on the heart-rate tiles. Warm-up is the switch and the minutes on that same row, 0–10, 5 by default. Session Setup, in the header, keeps the guards, the keys, the microphone, and the backup.

The session buttons are one full-width row: Play or Pause, Stop, Edged, Came Early, and Force Orgasm. Closing the Player hides the picture and leaves that row. On a playing video the readout at the top of the picture stays up, and the buttons fade after a few seconds until the pointer moves or the screen is tapped.

A session on the dashboard

  1. Connect a monitor and one toy from the row. The pulse stays -- until a beat arrives.
  2. Set resting heart rate, single max, and dual max on the tiles. Resting starts at 70 and can be typed, from 30 to 250. Dual max stays above it. On the live 1.1.3 site, resting is assumed at 70 and is not a field.
  3. Choose Fixed, Mystery, or Endless, and set warm-up or switch it off. On, the stroke stays in the lower half and opens across those minutes. Off is immediate. A funscript does not use this warm-up.
  4. Tap either card. Both lists open. Leave the play style and the ending you want, and the lists close.
  5. Play starts when a monitor and a toy are connected and the pulse is fresh. Stop ends the session and clears adaptive decay for that run. Edged, Came Early, and Force Orgasm sit on that same row.

Edged is on the row before a session starts, while one is running, and after a pause. A tap before the session starts does nothing. During a session it stops the toys the way Pause does, counts an edge the pulse has not already counted, and does not add a pause. That count is for the session and for No Nut November. During Calibration, Edged saves the heart rate you are on as the single or dual max and eases the toys down, before an orgasm. The orgasm is still The app / Finished me.

The Oracle waits for the Mystery minimum before it will finish you. A Fixed length unlocks halfway through. Endless has no minimum.

The Player

Player is in the header. On a phone the files come first. Until a video is chosen, the picture is a short placeholder that says to choose one in the file panel. Choose MP4, M4V, WEBM, MKV, MOV, or OGV, or drop the files. A pasted address is not on this build. MP4 (H.264) and WebM play in the most browsers. MKV and MOV play where this browser can decode them.

On a wide window the picture and the file panel each take half the width, and the two cards meet at the same height. The scrub, the time, Theater, and Fullscreen sit on one line under the picture. Theater widens the video inside the page. Fullscreen fills the screen. Stats Overlay is on. Heart rate, edges, and both toys can stay on the picture. The overlay does not change how the toys follow a file.

The session row stays under that picture: Pause, Stop, Edged, Came Early, and Force Orgasm. Closing the Player hides the picture and the file panel, and leaves the row. Play Style and Session Endgame then sit side by side again. The heart-rate tiles, including Length and Warm-up, do not move.

Primary and Secondary each start on EdgeLoop. Tap to configure. Inside, choose EdgeLoop or a funscript. Clear sits with the funscript you loaded. Primary is the stroker. Secondary is the other toy. A .funscript is the stroker and a .v0.funscript is the other toy. With no funscript, that tap opens Play Style and Session Endgame, so the stroke you are about to run is the thing under the channel. Funscript morphing stays folded until a funscript is loaded, and it closes again while a session is running. The file panel stays the files. The shaping is there when you are choosing it.

The two morphs:

  • Shorten and skip. A mild file opens up while your pulse is below the edge, and the strokes shorten as you get close. At the edge the toy skips strokes and the video keeps going. A climax mark asks for that opening. A pulse that is already too high still pulls the file back.
  • Keep the script. The strokes stay the shape in the file. Your pulse only turns them down. A pause in the file is a real stop.

The session warm-up does not shrink a funscript, and the stroke you had selected does not leave its range on the toy. The file uses the full travel you set. The primary meter is how much of the file the toy may play. 100% is the whole file, inside that travel. Intensity can turn that down, or back up to 100%. It does not open the file past the travel range.

Intiface linear axes and T-Code L0 play each stroke. The Handy plays each stroke when Beat sync is on, the firmware is 4 or later, and a funscript is loaded. Beat sync lives in The Handy settings, off until you turn it on. Off, The Handy follows the speed and depth of the moment. The first time you turn it on, it asks. While it is on, the next few seconds of stroke positions go to Handy's servers. The video, the file name, and your heart rate stay on this device. Connect a toy has the connection key and the travel range.

Under the picture, the scrub bar is a heat map. Green is a pause, red is fast. A gold mark is a climax. It uses three times, on the Funscript morphing panel:

  • Pre-Climax Ramp Up, 0–180 seconds, 45 by default. How long the toys spend climbing before the mark. Zero lets a mark release the limiter at the mark instead of climbing for most of a minute. The hold after the mark already ignores the pulse cap for Climax Duration.
  • Climax Duration, 5–180 seconds, 20 by default. How long they stay there.
  • Post Climax Ramp Down, 0–180 seconds, 15 by default. How long they ease back into the script. Zero on the ramp or the ease is immediate.

A mark does not end the session. Several marks: the strongest one wins. Marks for a script stay on this device for that script. A gold mark on a video with no funscript aims EdgeLoop at that time: Play Style still runs, and both channels lift toward full across the same three times.

Offset appears once a primary funscript is loaded. Each tap, and the [ and ] keys, moves the strokes 50 ms earlier or later than the picture. Those keys are listed in Session Setup, on the Keys tab.

If Play does nothing, the player says what it is waiting for: a heart-rate monitor, a toy, a fresh pulse, a script, or the video. Scrubbing still works.

Two climax numbers

Single max is the heart rate where you climax with one toy. The default is 140. Dual max is the heart rate where you climax with both a primary and a secondary live. The default is 125. When both are live, the ceiling is the dual number, and the cockpit shows a DUAL MAX badge. One toy live, and the ceiling is the single number.

Resting heart rate is a tile on the heart-rate card. It starts at 70. The curve in How a toy is driven runs from the resting number you set up to the pullback mark. A lower resting rate, down to 30, gives the first climb a real band when your pulse is already near 70. A pulse that only climbs a little still works when the climax number sits just above the highest reading you actually see.

Pullback, in Session Setup on the Guards tab, is 90 to 100 percent of the working ceiling, default 100. At 100 the mark is the number you typed. At 95 the toys ease off early. The edge counter still waits for the climax number itself, and releases 5 BPM below it.

Prostate-heavy sessions often edge in the 80s or 90s. A single max around 92–95 is what people have used there, in place of 140.

Calibration

Calibrate is a tile on the heart-rate card. It is a run whose job is to write one of those numbers.

  • The first run is the primary toy alone. Tap Edged when you are on the edge, before it finishes you. That saves the heart rate you are on and eases the toys down. If it does finish you, tap The app / Finished me. That saves the orgasm.
  • A later run with both toys live saves the dual max the same way. Edged writes the heart rate you are on. Finished me writes the orgasm.
  • The button follows whichever devices are on. Primary only replaces the single max. Both replace the dual max. Running it again replaces the number you already have.
  • The speed keeps climbing. Each edge raises the mark by 1 BPM, slowly enough that half an hour is still a build. Crawl and Full Stop do not park the toys, and the run does not end when you cross your old max.
  • Adaptive ceiling decay stays off for the run, so the climb is not pulled back. The decay setting itself is left alone for every other mode.
  • Changing the play style keeps the calibration running. Choosing another endgame, pressing Calibrate again, or Stop leaves it.

Saying you came does not cut the toys on the spot. They ease down. The length is Ease down after orgasm on the Guards tab, 0–180 seconds, 45 by default. 0 cuts immediately. The landing is Crawl or Full Stop, whichever you set for the ceiling. Then the session stops. In Survival, the ease-down is followed by the climb and the warm-up starting over, and the session timer keeps going, so one session can hold more than one orgasm.

Play Style

One stroke at a time. With no game selected, that stroke runs on its own, and the endgame is what the timer does. Global intensity, and which style is selected, are not restored when you reload. A fresh page that brought back 150 percent intensity, or Ruin & Leak, would be a motor decision you did not make this time. Set them for the session you are in.

Simple Ramp

This is the plain stroke. It used to be called Classic Tease, then Primary Ease. Speed starts high and ramps down as your pulse climbs toward the heart rate you set. Strokes stay the full length. Close to that heart rate, it sometimes stops for a beat and then picks the stroke back up. Crawl or Full Stop still decides the ceiling.

Finisher

It works to hold you at the heart rate you set. Under that number the stroke pushes. As your pulse arrives it eases off, and on the mark it holds instead of sitting at full speed. Crawl and Full Stop do not replace that hold. Room noise does not speed it up. The climb follows the pulse the sensor measured.

Prostate Milker

The stroker eases off as you climb and the internal toy takes over. Close to the heart rate you set, the stroker runs a fast stretch, stops for a beat, and climbs back. The internal toy rests on its own timing.

Glans Protector

The stroke stays full, and quicker than Simple Ramp, until you are close. Then it shortens toward the base. At the ceiling the stroke is the bottom 35 percent of your range. The secondary stays low.

Head Play

The stroke stays full until you are close, then climbs toward the head. Speed comes down as the stroke gets shorter, and the stroke opens back up when the pulse drops.

Ultimate Milker

The pattern changes with your pulse: long and steady, then long-slow against short-fast. Close to the heart rate you set, it runs a long fast stretch, stops for a beat, then climbs back up. The internal toy stays with you through the climb. That fast chapter does not open in the middle of the band.

Bratty Tease

Experimental. The climb matches Ultimate Milker until you are close. Then it stays up: a one-second dip, then the fast stroke again, several times, and a longer stretch that still dips after. At the ceiling that pattern keeps going instead of stopping.

Ruin & Leak

The stroker keeps moving through the edge. After about 12 seconds continuously on the pullback mark it stops dead for 18 seconds and the other toy drops to 18 percent, so ejaculation can happen without a full orgasm. Crawl and Full Stop do not govern that ride or that stop. Force Orgasm still overrides it.

Session Endgame

Climax, Soft Landing, Strict Denial

These are the ending when no game is on. Climax surges the toys to 100 percent when the timer runs out. Soft Landing eases them down for 45 seconds. Strict Denial stops everything. The play style you picked is the stroke until that timer.

Survival

The speed starts low and takes about half an hour to get hard on its own. Crossing your max does not end the run and does not park the toy. A session length does not end it either. Each edge counted while Survival is on raises the mark by 1 BPM and adds a little speed, up to 40 BPM past the working ceiling. Edges from before you switched it on do not count. Decay does not fight the climb. Press Stop when you are done.

While Survival is selected, Came Early reads The app / Finished me. That eases the toys down, then the climb and the warm-up start over. The session timer keeps running. Your typed climax number stays as typed.

Edge Training

The session pulls you to the ceiling and treats a timed hold as the goal. The hold is 5–90 seconds, default 15. The count is 1–20 holds, default 5. Both sit on the card. Drop before the hold finishes and it does not count. After the count, the session offers to finish you.

The hold, and the recover after it, obey Crawl or Full Stop. Tapping Force Orgasm yourself suspends the training rather than completing it: the count stays where it was, and the hold clock freezes. Cancelling Force Orgasm hands the training back. Cancelling it after the training has already finished starts a fresh set. The count goes back to zero. The next hold does not immediately finish you.

The Oracle

It holds you on the edge and then decides how the session ends. The stroke is whichever play style you picked. The speeds are the Oracle's. It keeps the finish you last left on Climax, Soft Landing, or Strict Denial, and the row of those three stays hidden while The Oracle is the card.

It will not climax or deny you before a minimum. With Mystery, that minimum is the low number you typed, including when the hidden target lands on that minimum, and including a Mystery whose two boxes hold the same number. With a Fixed length, the minimum is halfway. Endless has no minimum.

After that, each 15 second hold can end the session, more often as you near the target. At the target it uses that saved finish. A Soft Landing is teased down. Climax and Strict Denial do what they say.

No Nut November

A daily edge quota between a start date and an end date. It can be used in any month. The day's count, the dates, the edges per day, Add a missed day, and Clear sit on this card. There is no denial percent. The app knows which day of that window it is. A day you do not open the app adds that day's edges to the next day you do, and adds 15 seconds of hold, up to 60, that you have to stay on the edge before the edge counts. At the quota the toys ease down and the session ends. There is no orgasm. Clear drops the piled-up days and today's progress. The dates and the edges per day stay.

Guards

Session Setup, Guards tab. The guards are independent of the play style and the endgame, except where an endgame has said it does not use a particular guard. Calibration and Survival ignore the park. Ruin ignores it for its own ride. Finisher holds on the mark instead of parking. Decay stays off during Calibration and does not fight Survival's climb. Stop clears the decay for that run, because the decay is the session's edge count.

  • At the ceiling. Full Stop parks the primary at 0. Crawl is a number, 1 to 30 percent, 10 by default. The intensity slider still scales that crawl. On the live 1.1.3 site, Crawl is fixed at 10 percent.
  • Pullback. 90–100 percent of the working ceiling, default 100. The cockpit shows a HOLD TO badge and a line on the chart when this is not 100.
  • Stall guard. Optional, and only during a crawl. Allow on the edge is 3–120 seconds, default 20: how long the pulse may sit at the mark before the primary is cut. Pause the primary is 2–60 seconds, default 8: how long that halt lasts. Then crawl resumes. Turn it off to stay on crawl until you recover, Force Orgasm, or STOP. The secondary keeps running. The banner during the halt names what the mode you are actually in will do when the pause ends. In Ruin & Leak the lockout keeps the primary at 0 whatever the pause does.
  • Signal watchdog. Always on. The timing is on Heart-rate monitors. Past the timeout, motors stop and the session pauses. Start and Resume need a usable reading younger than the timeout. The transport reads WAITING FOR PULSE rather than driving the toys on a frozen heart rate.
  • Auto-resume. On by default. The session resumes once readings are back. Off, and it stays paused until you press RESUME.
  • Adaptive ceiling decay. Every few edges the ceiling drops, so a long session does not keep the same max after you have fatigued. The interval is 1–10 edges, default 2. The drop is 1–5 BPM, default 2. The floor is 80–130, default 105. The floor can stop the decay. It cannot raise a climax number you typed below it. Nothing can push the working ceiling below resting plus 15 BPM, or above the climax number you typed. The DECAY badge shows the amount currently applied.
  • Ease down after orgasm. 0–180 seconds, default 45. Covered under calibration above. Cancelling Force Orgasm uses the same ease. STOP does not. STOP cuts.

Warm-up is a tile on the heart-rate card, described above. It is not on this tab.

Force Orgasm

Force Orgasm is a temporary lift. It does not rewrite the climax number you typed.

The motors ease up from the speed they were just sent, over about 28 seconds, and they keep changing speed and stroke length at the top instead of sitting on one flat maximum. The ceiling climbs 1 BPM per second while it is on, for up to 60 seconds, so the pulse can run past the max you typed until you finish. The first second does not jump.

While it is on, the edge you are sitting on is frozen. The lift moves the mark up with the ceiling, so the unchanged pulse is not read as a release, and cancelling it cannot count an edge you did not have. Cancelling eases the toys down to Crawl or Full Stop over the ease-down time. It does not snap back to full speed.

In Edge Training, tapping it suspends the training. It does not complete the set.

Keys

Keys are a tab in Session Setup. Space starts or pauses. Escape stops, and leaves theater or fullscreen before it stops the session. Left is Came Early. Right is Force Orgasm. Up and down pulse the VacuGlide valves when that toy is connected. [ and ] move a loaded script 50 ms. Click a binding to change it with a key or a controller button. Space still pauses when a number such as Single max is focused.

What this browser remembers

Session Setup is saved in this browser: both climax numbers, resting heart rate, the session length and its times, the endgame, the warm-up, every guard, the cue lines, and the microphone settings. Lowering your ceiling by hand during a session is kept, so a reload does not hand the toys a limit you had already decided was too high. Climax marks for a script stay on this device for that script.

A backup export is the same set, plus the learned offset if you have one, the Handy role and speed cap, and your saved Intiface and T-Code maps. The Handy connection key is left out unless you tick the box. That key can drive the Handy from anywhere, with no password. The file with the key in it is named so you can see which one you wrote. Import is refused while a session is running. An import never connects a toy by itself.

On dev.edgeloop.app, History has a session log switch. It is off until you turn it on, including when no session is stored yet. After a session, that row offers a JSON download: the pulse once a second, what the toys were sent, the settings, and the make and model of the toys that were on. The file stays in this browser. It leaves out a Handy key, a device token, a Bluetooth address, a video address, and a script file name. edgeloop.app does not record it.

Session history stays out of the backup. It is a record of the session, and it is the largest thing in storage. The funscript download is the way to take a recorded stroke with you.

Cue lines show on the dashboard. The browser does not speak them. Empty a box to mute that cue. Reset defaults asks first, and brings the factory lines back.
