What a page will claim

A number on these pages is a number the program uses, or a report from someone who has run the app. The two are labeled differently.

The heart-rate table is the second kind. It is what people have gotten working, including the author's daily setup (a Pixel Watch 2, The Handy on Wi-Fi, EdgeLoop and Intiface Central on a Pixel Tablet). It is not a bench test of every strap on the market. A monitor that advertises the standard Bluetooth Heart Rate service can be tried even if it is missing from the table. One that does not advertise that service cannot, and no setting in the app changes that.

There is no accuracy percentage here for any monitor. EdgeLoop does not have one. Accuracy says what the page actually does with the number it is given, and why a chest strap and a watch steer the toys differently.

Where the program runs

The session is decided in the browser tab. There is no EdgeLoop account and no EdgeLoop server choosing a speed. How a toy is driven is the long version: the clocks, the curve, and the command each kind of toy actually receives.

A partner link is the exception to "nothing leaves the machine." Share Control introduces the two browsers through PeerJS, and the session commands then go from one browser to the other. That path is A partner on another device.
