Where the numbers come from

Some numbers are settings EdgeLoop itself uses, like a timeout. Others are what someone reported after a real session. The heart-rate table is that second kind. It is not a lab test of every strap on the market.

If a monitor broadcasts the standard Bluetooth heart-rate service, you can try it even when it is not in the table. If it does not, EdgeLoop cannot see it. No setting changes that.

There is no accuracy percentage for any monitor. We do not have one. Accuracy explains what EdgeLoop does with the number it is given, and why a chest strap and a watch feel different in a session.

Where it runs

The session happens in your browser tab. There is no EdgeLoop account, and no EdgeLoop server choosing a speed. How a toy is driven is the longer version: the clocks, the curve, and the command each toy actually receives.

Share Control is the exception. It introduces the two browsers, and then the commands go from one browser to the other. That is a partner on another device.

If something here is wrong
